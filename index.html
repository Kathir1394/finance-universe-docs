Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Thank you for using Finance Universe ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal financial data on our servers.

Finance Universe is designed as a local-first application. All data you enter into the app (including transaction details, budget information, account balances, and payment reminders) is stored locally on your device using an internal SQLite database. We do not have access to this data.

2. Data Export and Import

Finance Universe provides functionality to export your financial data to Excel (.xlsx) files and import data from them. This feature is designed to give you full control and ownership over your data. The exported files are generated locally on your device. When you choose to share or save these files, they are handled strictly by your device's standard system sharing mechanisms. We do not intercept, transmit, or upload these files to any external servers.

3. Receipt Scanning

The application includes an optional receipt scanning feature that uses on-device optical character recognition (Google ML Kit) to extract information from images you select or photograph. This processing happens entirely on your physical device. The images you scan are never uploaded to the cloud or shared with third parties.

4. Permissions

The application requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its core functionality:

  • Storage / Photos / Files: Required only if you choose to save exported Excel backup files to your device storage or select existing receipt photos from your gallery for scanning.
  • Camera: Required only if you choose to take a photo of a physical paper receipt directly within the app for scanning.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to deliver bill payment reminders and budget alerts that you configure. All notifications are scheduled and triggered completely on-device without remote push servers.
  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): Required on Android 14+ so that scheduled payment reminders alert you punctually at your chosen due time without system-imposed delays.
  • Device Boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Required exclusively to re-register your scheduled local payment reminders when your device is restarted.

We do not request or access sensitive data such as your contacts, SMS messages, phone call logs, microphone, or precise location.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may use open-source third-party libraries for standard technical functionality (such as local SQLite database drivers, charting, and local notification scheduling). These libraries run locally on your device and do not collect personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Data Security

Because all your financial records and reminders are stored exclusively on your device, data security relies on your physical device security. We recommend keeping your device protected with a secure passcode, PIN, or biometric lock.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or data practices, please contact us via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/Kathir1394/FinanceUniverse.
